## Features

### Farm records
- Log deaths, feed, water and weight each day in a few taps.
- Flock count, mortality, feed per bird and FCR are worked out automatically.

### Health
- Health scan: photograph a sick bird or droppings and get clear next steps.
- Vaccine and treatment records, with what is due next.
- Health scan is guidance only. For urgent cases, call a vet. Always say this when a post mentions the health scan.

### Hub Expert
- Ask any poultry question, any hour.
- Answers use the flock's age and records.

### Money
- Income, spending and profit by cycle or by year.
- Export an Excel report for an accountant.
- Farm calculators: chick orders, feed cost, break-even price, and more. TODO: list every calculator by name.

### Learn
- Short video and reading courses.
- Certificates that can be shared and verified at poultrixhub.com/verify.
- Some courses are paid.

### Marketplace
- Buy feed, chicks and equipment, and track the order to the farm.
- Sell your own products.

### Account
- Two-step login.
- Events, receipts, discounts and store credit.

- Any farmer name, testimonial, quote or result.
- That the app diagnoses or treats disease, or replaces a vet.
- Any income or profit promise ("double your profit", "earn more").
