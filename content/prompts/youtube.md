# YouTube Shorts

- Make a 20 to 30 second vertical story with a complete payoff.
- Put the central promise or conflict in the first 2 seconds.
- Use 3 to 5 visual beats with no filler or repeated text.
- Title-style headline under 70 characters. Description under 140 words.
- End with one specific call to action: follow, visit, watch, learn, book, or buy.
- Add 3 to 5 relevant hashtags.
