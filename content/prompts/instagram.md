# Instagram posts (carousels and Reels)

- Audience: younger farmers and agripreneurs who follow farming creators.
- Hook: carousels live or die on slide 1. The visual headline is the hook, under 8 words.
- Slides: each slide one idea, under 20 words. Cover, tension, useful steps, payoff, then install card.
- Visual hierarchy: lead with one bold claim, one contrast, or one decision. Never crowd a slide with explanation.
- Caption: 30 to 90 words, opening with the hook. Links are not clickable in captions, so say "link in bio" and "search PoultrixHub on Google Play".
- End with one call to action to install PoultrixHub.
- Hashtags: 5 to 8, mix broad (#PoultryFarming) and local (#UgandaFarmers #KenyaFarmers).
