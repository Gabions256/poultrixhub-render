# Facebook Page posts

- Audience: farmers and farm owners who scroll Facebook groups and pages on Android phones, often on slow data.
- Hook: the first line must make sense alone, because Facebook cuts the caption after about two lines.
- Caption: 40 to 120 words. Short paragraphs of one or two sentences. One practical takeaway.
- Hook architecture: choose one concrete problem, sharp contrast, or useful question. Do not begin with "Are you..." unless the question names a specific decision.
- Caption: 40 to 120 words. Open with the hook, add one practical takeaway, and use one short paragraph for the app connection.
- Tone: a capable neighbour who knows poultry. No hype, no ALL CAPS, at most one emoji.
- End with one line asking them to install PoultrixHub from Google Play (the link is added separately).
- Hashtags: 2 to 4, specific (#PoultryFarming #BroilerFarming #LayerFarming #UgandaFarmers).
