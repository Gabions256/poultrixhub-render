# TikTok videos (15 to 30 seconds, vertical)

- The video uses app screen recordings and on-screen text, no voice is required, and no trending audio.
- Hook: on-screen text in the first 2 seconds. Use a concrete problem, decision, or visual contrast from common knowledge or FACTS.
- Beats (visual.slides): 3 to 5 short on-screen text beats, under 10 words each. Build problem, reveal, action, useful payoff.
- Each beat must change the viewer's understanding. Do not write filler transitions or repeat the hook.
- The renderer adds the final install end card, so keep the final beat focused on the practical payoff.
- Caption: 1 to 3 short sentences plus hashtags. Say "search PoultrixHub on Google Play".
- End with one call to action to install PoultrixHub.
- Hashtags: 3 to 5 (#PoultryTok #FarmTok #ChickenFarming #PoultryFarming).
